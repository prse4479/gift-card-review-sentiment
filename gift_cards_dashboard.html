Gift Card Review Classifier — Results

A text-only sentiment classifier judged against a three-class rating-derived answer key, on a balanced sample of Amazon gift card reviews.

Headline

Scored on a balanced 150-review sample (≈50 per class, drawn at fixed random from the whole file). The model is text-only; the star rating is used solely to build the answer key afterwards.

The data — star-rating distribution

All 152,410 reviews, by the stars the reviewer actually gave. Bars are tinted by the answer-key class they belong to (4-5★ green, 3★ slate, 1-2★ red). This imbalance is exactly why the evaluation uses a balanced sample — otherwise the 3★ and 1-2★ tiers would be invisible.

Where each class's reviews went

The balanced sample (50 reviews per class): for each answer-key class, which label the model assigned to those 50 reviews. The green segment is the part answered right; the other segments are the failures — visible without any drilling.
